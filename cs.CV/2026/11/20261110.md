### How Hyperbolic Are Hyperbolic Vision-Language Models?
- **Arxiv ID**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13384v1
- **DOI**: None
- **Categories**: **cs.CV**, cs.AI
- **Links**: [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13384v1)
- **Published**: 2026-11-10 00:01:33+00:00
- **Updated**: 2026-11-10 00:01:33+00:00
- **Authors**: Freek Byrman, Partha Das, Stijn Verdenius, Pascal Mettes
- **Comment**: None
- **Journal**: Proceedings of the NeurIPS Workshop on Symmetry and Geometry in Neural Representations (NeurReps). PMLR, 2026
- **Summary**: Hyperbolic vision-language models (VLMs) have emerged as a rapidly growing area of research, reporting strong zero-shot performance alongside an interpretable embedding space in which radius orders representations by generality. But how hyperbolic are hyperbolic VLMs in practice? We investigate this at the exponential map, the single operation through which curvature enters an otherwise Euclidean network. We show that across MERU, HyCoCLIP, ARGENT, and PHyCLIP, the exponential map is well approximated by the identity function, i.e., pairwise distances and angles in the pre-map Euclidean space barely change under projection, and removing the map at inference leaves evaluation scores unchanged. Motivated by this observation, we train Euclidean VLMs on CC3M from scratch under the same hierarchy losses. Over roughly the final 90% of the training steps, the training statistics are nearly indistinguishable from those of their hyperbolic counterparts, and the resulting models match the evaluation scores and recover the same radial distributions. Our findings establish Euclidean space as a sufficient substrate for hierarchical vision-language learning as it is currently practiced, and motivate new paradigms in which hyperbolic geometry can deliver on its theoretical promise. Code is available at https://github.com/fbyrman/hyperbolic-vlms.

- **Summary**: Automated segmentation in multi-view echocardiography is essential for comprehensive cardiac assessment, yet remains challenging due to substantial anatomical and appearance variability across standard acoustic windows. - **Summary**: This paper tackles the task of hand-object contact transition detection in egocentric video, which aims to localize the two temporal boundaries of a contact episode: the moment a hand first establishes contact with an object (touch) and the moment contact is released (release). - **Summary**: SDR-to-HDR reconstruction is an ill-posed problem in which clipped highlights, crushed shadows, and quantization artifacts must be recovered while preserving the original content. ### TMAL: Topology-preserving Multi-offset Affinity Learning for Curvilinear Structure Segmentation
- **Arxiv ID**: http://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13511v1
- **DOI**: None
- **Categories**: **cs.CV**, physics.med-ph
- **Links**: [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.13511v1)
- **Published**: 2026-11-10 03:38:07+00:00
- **Updated**: 2026-11-10 03:38:07+00:00
- **Authors**: Suho Han, Soungsill Park, Kanghyun Ryu, Yoseob Han
- **Comment**: 15 pages, 11 figures, submitted to WACV 2027
- **Journal**: None
- **Summary**: Curvilinear structures such as retinal vessels and surface cracks need both pixel-level accuracy and structural continuity. Pixel-wise supervision does not model the connections between pixels, and topology-based losses still produce ordinary masks. Connectivity-based methods predict these connections directly, but they use binary connectivity targets over a fixed local range. Such labels give the same value to thin segments and to thick regions, and they cannot describe connections at larger offsets. To address these limitations, we propose Topology-preserving Multi-offset Affinity Learning (TMAL). TMAL uses soft affinities that emphasize thin regions near the boundary, and it predicts directional affinities at several offsets to reach further. It then combines these affinities with weights that come from the decoder features, so local continuity and wider context are used together. An auxiliary segmentation branch adds region-level supervision and keeps the overlap accuracy high. On the retinal vessel dataset DRIVE and the crack inspection dataset DeepCrack, TMAL reaches the highest Dice, IoU, and clDice scores, and it greatly reduces the connected-component error $Δβ_0$. Code is available at https://github.com/suho-han/TMAL.
